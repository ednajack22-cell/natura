# Competitor landscape: English-language small-space and urban food-growing sites (US first, then UK)

Research method note: done on 2026-10-23 with web search. Direct page fetches failed for several competitor domains (DNS errors for gardenerspath.com, balconygardenweb.com, nicheranger.com and google.co.uk in this environment), so many findings rest on search-result summaries of those pages rather than a full read. Where that is the case it is said. No traffic figures were invented; third-party estimates are labelled as such.

## 1. Who are the leading sites and brands, what angle does each take, and how do they make money?

### Takeaway
The field splits into (a) large media groups with general home/garden sites (Future plc: Gardening Know How, Homes & Gardens; People Inc., formerly Dotdash Meredith: The Spruce, Better Homes & Gardens), (b) creator-led brands that moved from ads/affiliate into selling products (Epic Gardening), (c) independent niche sites with ads and affiliate links (Gardener's Path, Balcony Garden Web), (d) software/education models (GrowVeg planner subscriptions, Joe Lamp'l courses), and (e) non-commercial authorities (RHS, Cooperative Extension) plus Reddit/YouTube. Only Balcony Garden Web is explicitly small-space; nobody found combines small-space + food + US zone/frost timing as its core angle.

### Cited Findings
**Epic Gardening (US, creator brand, general gardening with roots in small-space/hydroponics)**
- Started as a hydroponics blog by Kevin Espiritu with about $50, later rebranded into a broad gardening brand — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/50-startup-costs-earning-73m-yearly-how-kevin-espiritu-built-online-gardening-empire-1724625); [The Publish Press](https://thepublishpress.com/staffpicks23-kevinespiritu)
- Google's AdSense/publisher "story" on Epic Gardening says ad revenue was used as seed funding to expand into new revenue streams, including products; Espiritu says ads cover niche articles that have no products to sell — [Google Ads publisher story (search summary; page could not be fetched)](https://www.google.co.uk/ads/publisher/stories/epic_gardening/)
- Moved into e-commerce around 2019 (seeds, then raised beds, seed-starting trays, grow bags) and bought the seed company Botanical Interests, which sells wholesale through a network reported at 4,500+ independent garden centres and stores — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/epic-gardening/); [Recall summary of interview](https://www.recall.it/summary/online-business/he-built-a-dollar27myr-business-from-a-plant-blog)
- Revenue figures are self-reported in interviews and inconsistent: $2.8M (2020) and $7.3M (2021) in one profile; "nearly $30M" with only ~$1–1.5M from non-product income in another; "$45M annual revenue" in a podcast description — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/50-startup-costs-earning-73m-yearly-how-kevin-espiritu-built-online-gardening-empire-1724625); [Recall summary](https://www.recall.it/summary/online-business/he-built-a-dollar27myr-business-from-a-plant-blog); [Podwise episode page](https://podwise.ai/episodes/2055060). Treat as unverified estimates.

**Gardening Know How (US, general gardening, Q&A-style evergreen library)**
- Founded 2007, based in Ohio; acquired by Future plc, announced February 2023; Future said it strengthens its Homes category and US reach and would monetise the evergreen content "through its proprietary technology and SEO expertise" — [InPublishing](https://inpublishing.co.uk/articles/future-acquires-gardening-know-how-21620); [Future plc AGM trading statement, 8 Feb 2023](https://futureplc.com/investor-news/8-february-2023-agm-trading-statement/)
- Future's statement cites a Comscore audience of 1.1m and says it moved Future from 11th to 10th in the US Homes market (Comscore figure as reported by Future); a deal database states ~16,000 articles and "180 million annual users" (third-party, unverified) — [Future plc](https://futureplc.com/blog/8-february-2023-agm-trading-statement); [PrivSource](https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/rJSr6V)

**The Spruce / Better Homes & Gardens (People Inc., formerly Dotdash Meredith; general home incl. gardening; programmatic ads + commerce)**
- CEO Neil Vogel said ~60% of Dotdash + Meredith traffic came from Google in 2021 and about a third now; the company reported declining core sessions and tied part of it to weaker search referrals and AI Overviews — [Status News](https://www.status.news/p/neil-vogel-dotdash-meredith-google-ai); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/dotdash-meredith-reports-google-ai-overviews-impact-traffic-in-q1-earnings/); [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/people-inc-says-who-needs-google/)
- Detailed.com documents how a few media groups (including Dotdash Meredith and Future) own many sites dominating Google results across niches — [Detailed.com](https://detailed.com/google-control/comment-page-5/)

**Gardener's Path (US, independent general gardening, food + ornamentals)**
- Operated by Ask the Experts LLC (2020 job posting lists "Ask the Experts LLC / Gardener's Path"); a third-party brand profile says it monetises partly through affiliate links. Own disclosure page could not be fetched — [ProBlogger job listing](https://problogger.com/jobs/?p=43036); [LogoKit](https://logokit.com/brands/gardenerspath.com); [Gardener's Path About](https://gardenerspath.com/about-us)

**Balcony Garden Web (small-space specialist, ornamentals + edibles)**
- Describes itself as "solely dedicated to gardening" with a focus to "educate, inform and share" — [Trustpilot listing](https://trustpilot.com/review/balconygardenweb.com). No media kit or ad-network statement was found. Its own About page could not be fetched.
- A self-published-style expert profile names a founder (Sanchit Sharma); unverified — [PressHook](https://presshook.com/experts/sanchit-sharma)

**GrowVeg (UK/US, food growing, software subscription)**
- Revenue from an online Garden Planner subscription with a 7-day trial, sowing/planting reminder emails and crop-rotation features; bulk pricing for organisations; separate regional sites (.co.uk, .com, .com.au) — [GrowVeg pricing (UK)](https://www.growveg.co.uk/subscribeinfo.aspx); [GrowVeg pricing (US)](https://www.growveg.com/subscribeinfo.aspx); [GrowVeg bulk](https://www.growveg.co.uk/subscribebulk.aspx)
- GrowVeg's developer also built Charles Dowding's planner app, sold at £25/year — [Charles Dowding shop](https://shop.charlesdowding.co.uk/products/charles-dowdings-garden-planner-app)

**Joe Lamp'l / joe gardener (US, personality-led education)**
- Presented by Rodale Institute as "one of the country's most recognized and trusted personalities in gardening" and teaches paid gardening courses there — [Rodale Institute courses](https://courses.rodaleinstitute.org/p/gardening-courses-for-our-partners)

**Reddit and forums**
- Reddit's Google visibility rose sharply from mid-2023 (Sistrix: +1,328% visibility since late July 2023; Ahrefs-based estimate 57M → 427M US Google visits July 2023–April 2024; Semrush-based 132M → 346M — all third-party estimates) — [WPShout](https://wpshout.com/reddit-parasite-seo/); [Glenn Gabe, GSQi](https://gsqi.com/marketing-blog/beyond-reddit-and-quora-google-hidden-gems-update-forums-surge/)

### Inferences
- The two biggest US general gardening libraries (Gardening Know How, The Spruce) sit inside large ad-funded media groups whose stated strategy is to reduce dependence on Google; a new independent site will not out-scale them on broad terms but can out-specialise them.
- The most successful independent (Epic Gardening) shows the content-to-products path: ads are a minor share of revenue once products exist. Natura's current model (AdSense + affiliate + Pinterest) is the "early-stage" version of that path.
- GrowVeg proves people pay for planning tools tied to local timing; Natura's existing frost/planner tools are in the same space.

### Gaps
- Could not verify ad networks (Mediavine/Raptive/etc.) for Epic Gardening, Gardener's Path or Balcony Garden Web: disclosure pages could not be fetched.
- Bonnie Plants, Homes & Gardens, RHS, University of Minnesota and Penn State Extension angles were not researched in this pass (no fetchable sources within the call budget). Known context, unsourced here: Bonnie Plants is a plant seller whose content supports retail sales; RHS is a UK charity funded by membership; Extension sites are non-commercial. Verify before using.
- No reliable, sourced traffic figures for any competitor beyond the labelled estimates above.

## 2. Which publishers were hit or helped by Google's Helpful Content and core updates (2023–2026), and what does it imply?

### Takeaway
No public SEO analysis found names large US gardening publishers as clear winners or losers. The broad pattern is well documented: the September 2023 Helpful Content Update hit many small niche/affiliate sites hard, forums and Reddit gained massively, and big publishers lost Google share to AI Overviews. One gardening-specific study links losses to anonymous, non-expert authorship.

### Cited Findings
- September 2023 HCU: publishers reported drops of 10–70%; the system is site-wide, so a large share of unhelpful pages can drag a whole domain down — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-helpful-content-updates-survive-thrive-432843); [Search Engine Land (HCU launch)](https://searchengineland.com/googles-new-helpful-content-update-targets-sites-creating-content-for-search-engines-first-387237)
- Marie Haynes reported sites hit by the September 2023 HCU had not recovered and some were hit again by later core updates — [Marie Haynes](https://www.mariehaynes.com/the-september-helpful-content-update-why-you-were-affected-and-what-you-can-do/)
- Gardening-specific: a Niche Ranger study of ~50 gardening sites profiled GetBusyGardening losing almost 200K ranking keywords and ~50% of SEO traffic; the unaffected comparison site had a published gardening author and big social presence; the author attributes the difference to visible real expertise and identifiable authors (search summary; page could not be fetched) — [Niche Ranger](https://nicheranger.com/the-gardening-fix/)
- A study of 7,105 niche sites found 80.1% lost some traffic Dec 2023–Aug 2024, without claiming direct causation — [Paul Teitelman](https://www.paulteitelman.com/a-6-month-study-of-the-potential-impact-of-googles-march-2024-helpful-content-update-on-niche-sites/)
- Search Engine Land describes "thousands of blogs from every possible niche down as much as 90%" — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/niche-blogging-new-google-reality-strategies-440595)
- A "best flowers" listicle (Country Living style) lost rankings after September 2023 as results shifted to commercial/transactional sites — [StrategiQ](https://strategiq.co/?p=51047)
- Forums/Reddit: Google's "hidden gems" change (rolled into fall 2023 core updates) drove forum visibility; Reddit was again a big winner of the November 2024 core update per Lily Ray; Glenn Gabe reported Reddit surging again in the March 2025 core update while many other forums dropped — [GSQi](https://gsqi.com/marketing-blog/beyond-reddit-and-quora-google-hidden-gems-update-forums-surge/); [Lily Ray on X](https://x.com/lilyraynyc/status/1863274472964251991); [Glenn Gabe, Mastodon](https://mas.to/@glenngabe/114234544156920576)
- Home category: March 2025 core update gainers included ThisOldHouse.com and HomeDepot.com; December 2025 analysis (Sistrix data) found retail/e-commerce brands among the biggest winners — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-march-core-update-early-observations-from-initial-rollout/542795/); [Amsive](https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/googles-december-2025-core-update-winners-losers-analysis/)
- June 2025 core update: YouTube the biggest US winner (Sistrix) — [Amsive](https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/june-2025-core-update-winners-losers-trends/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/data-providers-google-june-2025-core-update-was-a-big-update-459226)
- March 2026 core update in Germany: 4 losers per winner; a German houseplant site (123zimmerpflanzen.de) down ~27% — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-march-core-update-left-4-losers-for-every-winner-in-germany/571639/)
- People Inc. says Google now sends about a third of its traffic versus ~60% in 2021 — [Status News](https://www.status.news/p/neil-vogel-dotdash-meredith-google-ai)

### Inferences
- For Natura: visible first-hand growing (own photos, own containers, named author), cited Extension/RHS sources and a tight topical focus are the defensible signals; mass-produced generic plant pages carry site-wide risk.
- Reddit and YouTube take a big share of "real experience" queries; Pinterest and email matter as non-Google channels (consistent with Natura's Pinterest focus).
- Retail brands winning in late 2025 suggests "best X" product queries are hard for a small publisher; informational how-to and timing queries are a better fit.

### Gaps
- No public named list of US gardening domains hit/helped by 2023–2025 updates was found (Sistrix/Ahrefs category tables were not accessible).
- Epic Gardening's and Gardening Know How's own visibility trends are not documented in any source found.

## 3. What content gaps could a new site own?

### Takeaway
Evidence is partial but points to urban-practical questions (balcony load limits, landlord/building rules) being answered mainly in forums and general news pieces, not in structured food-growing guides. The combined angle "small space + food + US zone/frost timing + Extension/RHS sources" was not found as the core positioning of any competitor reviewed.

### Cited Findings
- Balcony weight is a real, recurring tenant question: a forum thread shows a renter planning 12 fabric pots (~60–70 lb each wet), a landlord objecting, and the tenant reasoning from a 40 psf live-load code — [Penny Arcade forum](https://forums.penny-arcade.com/discussion/235534/balcony-gardening-and-landlord)
- A Weather Network article notes some balconies are designed for non-permanent loads of only 40 lb/sq ft (citing BC Housing), that wet soil can exceed limits, and that building managers may ban railing planters or hanging pots — [The Weather Network](https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/weather/seasonal/how-you-can-transform-a-small-balcony-space-into-a-large-garden)
- Planning-by-local-timing is a paid product category (GrowVeg reminders, Charles Dowding planner) — [GrowVeg](https://www.growveg.com/subscribeinfo.aspx); [Charles Dowding shop](https://shop.charlesdowding.co.uk/products/charles-dowdings-garden-planner-app)

### Inferences
- Candidate gaps for Natura (to be validated with keyword and forum data): balcony load and lightweight-mix guidance; renter/HOA/landlord rules; rooftop wind; shade between buildings (hours of direct sun); year-round container food plans by USDA zone and frost date; community garden plot planning; realistic per-container yield expectations sourced from Extension.
- Balcony Garden Web is the closest small-space competitor but (from its self-description) is a general gardening site, not US-zone timed food growing.

### Gaps
- Reddit threads in r/containergardening, r/UrbanGardening, r/BalconyGardening could not be surfaced by the search tool; no quantified evidence of unanswered questions was collected.
- No evidence collected on whether yield guidance or community-garden plot planning is thinly covered; needs a SERP review.

## 4. How strong is competition on broad terms vs long-tail?

### Takeaway
No reliable keyword difficulty data for "container gardening" or "grow your own food" was found. Indirect evidence (media-group dominance, retail winners, Reddit/YouTube surges) suggests broad heads are held by large domains; long-tail, location- and situation-specific questions are the realistic entry point.

### Cited Findings
- One keyword tool (Etsy-oriented, not Google) lists "gardening" at 135,000 monthly searches, KD 54; "home gardening" 33,100, KD 59; "container herb" 1,600, KD 38 — [RankHero: gardening](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/gardening); [RankHero: home gardening](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/home-gardening); [RankHero: container herb](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/container-herb). These are not Google figures; treat as rough.
- Large groups control many top results across niches — [Detailed.com](https://detailed.com/google-control/comment-page-5/)

### Inferences
- Target long-tail clusters such as "[crop] in containers zone [x]", "when to plant [crop] on a balcony [city/zone]", "balcony weight limit potting soil", where Extension sources give Natura an authority edge.

### Gaps
- Real Google volumes and difficulty for "container gardening", "grow your own food", "balcony vegetable garden" need Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword Planner access, which was not available.
