# Affiliate programs and Pinterest opportunities for small-space food growing (US first, then UK)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Method note: direct page fetching failed (DNS errors on amazon, brand sites, pinterest.com, ftc.gov), so every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages. Figures marked "(official)" were taken from the brand's or network's own page; "(third-party)" means an aggregator or blog. Re-check every rate on the live terms page before publishing or deciding.

## 1. Amazon Associates US and UK: rates by category and cookie

### Takeaway
On Amazon US, the categories that matter for this site pay little: about 3% for Lawn & Garden, Home, Outdoors and Tools, and 4.5% for Kitchen (off-site rates, third-party copies). Amazon UK appears to pay more for Home and Kitchen (6%). The official dated fee schedules could not be fetched, so these rates must be confirmed in Associates Central.

### Cited Findings
- US off-site standard rates per third-party copies: Kitchen 4.5% (same tier as Physical Books and Automotive); Home, Lawn & Garden, Home Improvement, Furniture, Pets, Toys 3% — [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/); [HM Marketing](https://helpingmerchants.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-by-category/); [Geniuslink historical guide](https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-historical-guide/)
- Conflict: one third-party source lists "Outdoor, Tools, Lawn & Garden: 4.5%", while most others put these at 3% — [search summary of 2026 guides, e.g. EcomChief](https://ecomchief.com/blogs/news-1/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-2026)
- Lawn & Garden was 8% before the April 2020 cuts. Older articles still quote 8% and are outdated — [Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-historical-guide/)
- Official Associates Central help page with the **onsite** (storefront/creator) fixed table: Furniture, Home, Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Pets 3.00%; Tools 1.75%; Kitchen 1.25%; Outdoors 0.75%. These are onsite rates, not the standard off-site rates a website earns — [Amazon Associates Central help (official)](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA)
- The seller "referral fee" (15% for Lawn & Garden) applies to marketplace sellers, not Associates. Do not confuse the two — [SellerKit](https://sellerkittools.com/blog/amazon-referral-fees-by-category)
- UK official compensation page (undated in the extract): Home, Home Improvement, Kitchen & Dining, Beauty 6%; Furniture and Power & Hand Tools 5.5%. No Lawn & Garden line was visible in the extract — [Amazon.co.uk Associates Standard Commission Income Statement (official)](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/welcome/compensation)
- Older UK table (March 2021, third-party copy): Home incl. Patio and Lawn & Garden at 7% (<£40k qualifying revenue) and 8% above. Outdated — [search summary via AmaLinks Pro](https://amalinkspro.com/?p=17337)

### Inferences
- A $40 self-watering planter at 3% earns about $1.20 per sale on Amazon US. Amazon is useful for its coverage of cheap items (pots, soil, trellises, seed starting) and its conversion rate, not for the rate itself.
- UK Amazon at about 6% for Home/Kitchen is roughly double the US rate for the same item types, if confirmed.

### Gaps
- The official dated US fee schedule (affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule) and the UK Lawn & Garden / Garden / DIY rate could not be retrieved (DNS failure). The date of the current schedule is unconfirmed.
- Cookie: the standard Amazon rule (24 hours after the click; 89 days if the item is added to the cart within those 24 hours) is widely known but was **not confirmed from a source in this session**. Verify it before quoting.

## 2. Specialist programs: network, commission, cookie, country

### Takeaway
Specialist brands pay much better than Amazon (typically 5–20%) with 30–45-day cookies. The best-documented are UK seed sellers on Awin (Thompson & Morgan 8%, Suttons 6%), Sarah Raven on Webgains (10% new customers) and US True Leaf Market on Awin (10–20% for content partners). Many US hardware and indoor-garden figures come only from third-party roundups and conflict with each other.

### Cited Findings

**Seeds, US**
- True Leaf Market (US): the brand page mentions the PepperJam network and "high commission rates", with no minimum referral requirement. Its Awin profile lists 10–20% for content and influencer partners (performance-tiered) and 2–5% for coupon partners. Takeads says "up to 2%" (conflict, probably a coupon-tier or outdated figure) — [True Leaf affiliate page (official)](https://trueleafmarket.com/pages/affiliate-program-trueleafmarket); [Awin profile 124042](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/124042); [Takeads](https://takeads.com/merchants/true-leaf-market-us/)
- Botanical Interests (US): a "Creator Community" program on Awin, with an Affiliate tier and a higher-commission Ambassador tier. The rate is not stated in the extract. Older third-party listings say 15% with a 45-day cookie on ShareASale (ShareASale has since been merged into Awin) — [Botanical Interests (official)](https://www.botanicalinterests.com/pages/creator-community-program); [ProAffiliatePartners (third-party)](https://proaffiliatepartners.com/affiliate-programs/herbal/botanical-interests)
- Eden Brothers (US): 2% payout, 30-day cookie on FlexOffers. The listing is about 600 days old and its status is unclear — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/eden-brothers-seed-company-affiliate-program/)
- Other seed sellers (old roundup, about 5 years): Growers Choice Seeds 20%, 30-day cookie; Seeds for Generations 10–20%, 180-day cookie — [3HUNDRD](https://3hundrd.com/seed-affiliate-programs)
- Johnny's, Baker Creek, Burpee, MIgardener: **not found** in this session.

**Seeds, UK**
- Thompson & Morgan (UK, Awin): 8% default, paid on the value after VAT and excluding delivery, 30-day cookie. No commission on "Just Pay Postage" offers or on the Tool Shed, Outdoor Living, Buildings & Fencing and Wild Birdfood categories. Only codes supplied by T&M may be promoted. FlexOffers shows 6.4% (4% on voucher sales) — [Awin profile 2283 (network)](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/2283); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/thompson-morgan-affiliate-program/)
- Suttons (UK, Awin): 6% default (after VAT, excluding delivery), 30-day cookie. Strongest in spring. The FlexOffers listing is deactivated (4.8%) — [Awin profile 25121](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/25121); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/suttons-affiliate-program/)
- Sarah Raven (UK, Webgains): 10% for new customers and 5% for existing ones (excluding postage and VAT), 30-day cookie. 0% may apply when a non-affiliate code is used. FlexOffers (deactivated) shows 8%/4% — [Webgains directory](https://www.webgains.com/public/en/directory/sarah-raven-affiliate-programme/); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/sarah-raven-affiliate-program/)
- Real Seeds (UK): **not found**.

**Indoor gardens and grow lights**
- AeroGarden (US): third-party sources say 7.5% with a 40-day cookie, one says "up to 12%", and the network is disputed (FlexOffers vs CJ) — [UpPromote directory](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/aerogarden/); [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/gardening-affiliate-programs/). **Status risk:** Scotts Miracle-Gro announced in late 2024 that the AeroGarden line would end at the start of 2025 — [Texas Standard](https://texasstandard.org/stories/aerogarden-scotts-miracle-gro-shutting-down-gardening-kit-discontinued/); [Brick Underground](https://www.brickunderground.com/improve/how-to-keep-AeroGarden-growing-after-company-shutdown). - Click & Grow: 10% and 45-day cookie per third-party roundups. An older figure says 10.4% and 50 days — [LinkWhisper](https://linkwhisper.com/gardening-affiliate-programs/); [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/gardening-affiliate-programs/)
- Gardyn (US): conflicting third-party figures (up to 10% with a 30-day cookie; 10.4% with 50 days; flat $100). No official terms found — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/gardening-affiliate-programs/); [Affiliate Marketer Training](https://www.affiliatemarketertraining.com/13-best-gardening-affiliate-programs/)
- Lettuce Grow, Rise Gardens: **not found** (no terms surfaced).
- Mars Hydro: the official US page gives 1% on promo-code sales (fixed from 5 June 2025). The UK and AU pages give 3–5%. One grower blog alleges unpaid creators (single author, about 2024) — [Mars Hydro US (official)](https://www.mars-hydro.com/affiliate-program); [Mars Hydro UK (official)](https://marshydroled.co.uk/affiliate-program/); [HowWeedGrow](https://www.howweedgrow.com/mars-hydro-affiliate-link-program-scam/)

**Planters (self-watering, vertical, raised)**
- Vego Garden (US): Impact network, free to join, review within 3–5 business days. The official page does not state a rate. A third-party guide says about 8% with a 30-day cookie — [Vego Garden (official)](https://www.vegogarden.com/pages/affiliate-program); [ThriversHub](https://thrivershub.com/gardening-affiliate-programs)
- GreenStalk (US): 5–10% commission per its official page. Network and cookie not in the extract — [GreenStalk (official)](https://greenstalkgarden.com/pages/affiliate-program)
- EarthBox: no program found on the major networks (undated) — [Knoji](https://earthbox.knoji.com/questions/earthbox-affiliate-programs/)
- Vegega (different company, raised beds): 10–20% advertised — [Vegega (official)](https://www.vegega.com/pages/affiliate-program)
- Bloem, Smart Pots, Root Pouch, Elho UK: **not found**.

**Composting**
- Vego composter (vego.com): requires at least 10,000 followers. The offer window ran from 21 October to 14 November 2024, so it has probably expired — [vego.com (official)](https://vego.com/pages/affiliate-program)
- Bokashi, worm farms, FCMP, Lomi: **not found**.

**Preserving**
- Harvest Right freeze dryers (US): 3–10% of the purchase price, 45-day tracking (iDev), monthly payouts with a $200 minimum — [Harvest Right (official)](https://harvestright.com/pages/affiliate-program)

### Inferences
- For a US-first site, the best candidates combine a high basket value with a published rate: Harvest Right (high-ticket, 3–10%), indoor gardens (Gardyn at about $899, if its rate is confirmed), Vego Garden via Impact, GreenStalk 5–10%, and True Leaf Market 10–20% (content tier).
- For UK readers, Awin covers T&M and Suttons, and Webgains covers Sarah Raven. Amazon UK at about 6% Home/Kitchen is competitive.
- Avoid relying on AeroGarden (product line ended or is uncertain) and on Mars Hydro US (1%, plus a non-payment allegation).

### Gaps

- Not found: Johnny's, Baker Creek, Burpee, MIgardener, Real Seeds, Lettuce Grow, Rise Gardens, Bloem, Smart Pots, Root Pouch, Elho, FCMP, Lomi, Bokashi, worm farms, Excalibur, Ball, Presto, rain barrels.

No gardening-specific Predicts trend and no official home & garden audience share were found.

### Cited Findings

## 5. Affiliate disclosure rules: FTC (US) and ASA/CMA (UK)

### Takeaway
Both regimes require a clear disclosure near the recommendation or link, not hidden in a footer. The FTC accepts plain wording such as "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post". The ASA expects "Ad" labelling, and has found "*affiliate" alone insufficient.

### Cited Findings
- FTC Endorsement Guides were revised in 2023. A commission is a material connection. A disclosure must be "difficult to miss" and easily understood. "Consumers may miss a disclosure at the bottom of a blog or page." Example wording: "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post". "Paid link" next to a link is adequate. "Affiliate link" alone may not be understood — [FTC: Endorsement Guides FAQ (official)](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking); [Manatt on the 2023 update](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-2)
- UK, CAP Code: marketing must be obviously identifiable. ASA affiliate guidance applies to affiliate content. Label with "Ad" ("(Ad)" or "#Ad"), in the title when a whole page is affiliate content, or on the relevant sections otherwise. A disclaimer at the bottom of a post is unlikely to be enough. The ASA has found "*affiliate" insufficient — [SeqLegal summary](https://seqlegal.com/digital-marketing-laws/affiliate-marketing-laws/); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-disclose-affiliate-links/)

### Inferences
- The site's current rule (disclosure before the first affiliate link, `rel="sponsored nofollow"`) is compatible with both regimes. For UK readers, consider adding an "Ad" label near affiliate blocks.

### Gaps
- ASA/CAP and CMA primary pages (asa.org.uk, gov.uk) were not retrieved, so the UK points rely on secondary summaries. No CMA-specific guidance was found in this session.
