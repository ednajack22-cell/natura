# Natura competitive and editorial dashboard analysis

Date: 11 August 2026  
Scope: public gardening guidance, plant-problem discovery, and professional editorial workspaces.

## Executive decision

Natura should not imitate a high-volume gardening magazine. Its strongest position is a compact, source-visible system that connects four reader tasks: choose a plant, understand its conditions, compare a symptom, and act at the right time. The administration should therefore prioritise decisions and blockers over decorative analytics.

## Comparable gardening experiences

| Reference | Pattern worth keeping | Natura response |
|---|---|---|
| [RHS growing guides](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/types/houseplants/growing-guide) | Quick facts lead into planting, care, propagation, and problems; ambiguous symptoms are presented as possible causes rather than instant diagnoses. | Keep essential facts near the top, connect every plant to diagnostic guides, and preserve observation-first language. |
| [RHS Grow](https://www.rhs.org.uk/rhsgrow/) | Plant collections, care prompts, journal entries, identification, and combinations form one ongoing gardening workflow. | Continue linking profiles, seasonal tasks, tools, and observation instead of publishing isolated articles. |
| [GrowVeg Garden Planner](https://www.growveg.com/garden-planner-intro.aspx) | A plan produces contextual calendars and recommendations; crop and problem information is available from the planning context. | Make launch coverage and structured relationships visible to editors; keep assumptions visible in every calculator. |
| [Epic Gardening problem guide](https://www.epicgardening.com/potato-problems/) | Clear author and horticultural reviewer, freshness date, contents, and problem-by-problem scanning. | Display editorial responsibility and freshness, then add concise symptom/cause/action sections and visible sources. |
| [Penn State container guidance](https://extension.psu.edu/container-vegetable-gardening-four-keys-to-success) | Container, growing mix, plant choice, light, drainage, water, and feeding are treated as one system. | Keep plant profiles practical for small spaces and avoid universal watering schedules. |

## Professional dashboard references

| Reference | Professional pattern | Natura implementation priority |
|---|---|---|
| [Sanity dashboard widgets](https://www.sanity.io/docs/studio/dashboard) | Project-specific widgets for recent, stale, or selected content. | Add a decision-oriented action centre instead of more generic counters. |
| [Contentful entry tasks](https://www.contentful.com/developers/docs/references/content-management-api/entry-tasks/) | Ownership, due date, status, and unresolved work can block publication. | Surface drafts, review items, SEO blockers, and missing public authors as direct queues. |
| [Storyblok workflows](https://www.storyblok.com/docs/manuals/workflows) | Drafting, reviewing, ready-to-publish stages with assignees and due dates. | Keep the existing author/reviewer separation and expose the next required action on the overview. |
| [Storyblok Visual Editor](https://www.storyblok.com/docs/manuals/visual-editor.html) | Draft preview, explicit status, responsive preview, comments, history, and scheduling share one editing context. | Retain preview/history controls and make their state more visible without rebuilding the editor. |
| [Strapi content management](https://strapi.io/content-management) | Structured content, live preview, releases, review workflows, and roles protect coordinated publishing. | Treat launch targets as a collection and show progress by content type, not only plant count. |

## Changes selected for Natura

1. Add an action centre to the main dashboard with direct links to review drafts, SEO blockers, missing public author profiles, and launch gaps.
2. Replace the plant-only launch panel with collection coverage across plants, diagnostics, guides, calendars, tools, reviews, and trust pages.
3. Keep recent content, but label it as context rather than the main work queue.
4. Show visible sources on public plant and diagnostic pages.
5. Start the launch collection with one complete plant profile and three connected diagnostic guides, all backed by named sources.

## Explicit non-goals

- No ranking promise or imitation of competitor traffic strategies.
- No automatic diagnosis from one symptom.
- No dashboard chart without an editorial decision attached to it.
- No public contributor biography until identity and expertise are approved.
- No broad content import or copied prose; Natura content remains original and source-informed.
